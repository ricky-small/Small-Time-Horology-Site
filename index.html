WHY SMALL TIME HOROLOGY

A collector's archive, not just a spreadsheet.

Small Time Horology grew out of a simple problem: a serious collection quickly becomes more than a list of brands and references.

Movements, case codes, photos, repair histories, purchase records, storage locations and research all deserve a place of their own.
